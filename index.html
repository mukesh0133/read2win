The Mystery of Green Valley School Fair

Green Valley School was very excited because the annual school fair was coming on Saturday, 15th June.

The principal, Mrs. Anita Sharma, announced that students from Class 1 to Class 5 would participate in different activities.

The school had 520 students in total.

Out of them: 110 students were from Class 1, 95 students were from Class 2, 105 students were from Class 3, 100 students were from Class 4, and 110 students were from Class 5.

The fair would begin at 10:00 AM and end at 5:00 PM.

There were many stalls planned for the fair: 8 food stalls, 5 game stalls, 3 book stalls, and 2 science project stalls.

The school invited 12 teachers and 20 parents to help manage the fair.

Class 4 students were given special responsibilities.

Riya and Arjun were selected as team leaders for Class 4.

Their teacher, Mr. Rajesh, divided 100 Class 4 students into 4 groups: Red Group – 25 students, Blue Group – 20 students, Green Group – 30 students, Yellow Group – 25 students.

The Red Group decorated the entrance gate using: 50 balloons, 20 paper flowers, and 15 ribbons.

The Blue Group managed the drinking water area. They arranged: 10 water jars, 200 paper cups, and 4 tables.

The Green Group prepared games for younger children. They organized Ring Toss, Balloon Shooting, and Lucky Draw.

The Yellow Group worked at the book stall.

The book stall sold: 40 story books, 35 drawing books, and 25 science magazines.

Each story book cost ₹50. Each drawing book cost ₹30. Each science magazine cost ₹40.

At 11:30 AM, the principal announced the start of the Treasure Hunt Competition.

There were 10 teams in the competition. Each team had 5 students.

The winning team received: 5 gold medals, a trophy, and chocolates.

At lunchtime, the food stalls became very crowded.

The most popular food item was samosa.

The food stalls sold: 320 samosas, 210 sandwiches, 180 juice boxes, and 150 ice creams.

One samosa cost ₹15. One sandwich cost ₹25. One juice box cost ₹20. One ice cream cost ₹30.

By 3:00 PM, the school had collected ₹18,500 from all stalls.

The principal decided to use the money for: new library books, sports equipment, and classroom plants.

At the end of the fair, the principal thanked everyone for their hard work.

Mrs. Sharma said, “Teamwork and honesty are more important than winning.”

All students clapped happily, and the fair ended successfully at 5:00 PM.
